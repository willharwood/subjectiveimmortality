      Transported to Mars times two (and divided by two)

The obvious question when we say that two physically identical people would have identical consciousnesses, how can that be, what could that split look like? Perhaps there is no problem: the consciousnesses are identical while the physical is identical, and then they change at the point that any change could matter. Making this point understandable is difficult. The way to understand how a person's consciousness could be duplicated or split is, ultimately, that it rests on a mistake on the nature of consciousness, one we will repeatedly meet. That there is conscious continuity. No: at any moment, an experience is created, that is it. There is no separate entity experiencing the experience who has an existence that outlasts it. The you that we instinctively believe in was born a moment ago and won't exist in a moment. I truly don't see any other solution.
